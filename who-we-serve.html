Mining and Natural Resources

From exploration stage concessions to production assets, we connect international mining companies and investment firms with high quality African mining opportunities. Our network includes direct relationships with mining ministries, concession holders, and geological experts across the continent. Ghana alone is Africa's number one gold producer, and our base of operations gives us unmatched access to the country's mining sector.

Energy and Renewable Power

Africa's energy sector presents some of the most compelling investment and operational opportunities on the planet. We support companies across the full spectrum from solar and wind projects to traditional power generation, helping navigate the regulatory frameworks, land rights, and grid connection agreements that define success in this space.

Solar Wind Power Generation Grid Infrastructure Off Grid Solutions

Healthcare and Medical Services

The demand for quality healthcare infrastructure and services across Africa continues to grow rapidly. We help medical device companies, pharmaceutical firms, health services providers, and digital health companies navigate the regulatory landscape and build the partnerships needed to deliver healthcare solutions at scale.

Medical Devices Pharmaceuticals Health Services Digital Health Hospital Infrastructure

Agriculture and AgriTech

With 60% of the world's uncultivated arable land, Africa's agricultural sector represents an enormous opportunity for international companies. We support everything from large scale farming operations to AgriTech platforms, connecting businesses with the land rights, distribution networks, and local partnerships needed to operate successfully.

Commercial Farming AgriTech Supply Chain Processing Export

Oil and Gas

Africa holds significant proven oil and gas reserves with new discoveries continuing to reshape the sector. We provide operational support and investment sourcing across the upstream, midstream, and downstream value chain, with particular expertise in the regulatory complexity and government relations that define success in African oil and gas markets.

Upstream Midstream Downstream Exploration Production

Africa's infrastructure gap represents one of the largest investment opportunities globally.
