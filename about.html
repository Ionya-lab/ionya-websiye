About Ionya

Built on relationships, not PowerPoint decks

We exist because international companies deserve a partner who actually operates in Africa, not one who flies in, delivers a report, and disappears.

Leadership

The person behind the network

Nana Adjei Sarpong

Founder and Principal Consultant

I was blessed with an opportunity that most people from where I grew up never get. I moved to America at a young age and was able to take full advantage of the resources available to me, earning my bachelors degree in the US and my masters in the UK. Along the way, from selling since high school onwards, I developed a deep understanding of relationships and what it takes to create genuine win win outcomes for everyone at the table.

But here is what drives me every single day: I know people across African countries who are far smarter than I am, far more capable, but who never got the same opportunity. Not because they lack talent or ambition, but because the infrastructure, the systems, the pathways simply don't exist where they are. That reality upsets me daily. It is not acceptable.

That is why Ionya exists. We are building something bigger than a consultancy. We are creating the infrastructure and the international connections so that people in these countries don't have to leave home to survive. They should only leave to visit, to explore, to choose, never because they have no other option. Every engagement we take on, every relationship we build, every deal we facilitate moves us closer to that reality.

We have an extraordinary network that spans the globe, and we intend to use it to create outcomes where everyone involved wins. That is not a slogan. It is how we have built every relationship that powers this company.

Our Name

Why "Ionya"

Our name draws from Iona, a small island off the coast of Scotland that became one of history's most influential centres of learning, community building, and cross cultural exchange. In the 6th century, a small group of people on that island transformed entire regions, not through force or extraction, but by building lasting relationships, strengthening local communities, and creating systems designed to outlast their builders.

That pattern, build rather than extract, strengthen rather than exploit, create systems that serve generations rather than quarters, is exactly what we believe Africa needs from international business. Ionya carries that principle into every engagement. We are not here for quick transactions. We are here to build infrastructure, relationships, and opportunities that create lasting prosperity for everyone involved.

How We Operate

Principles, not just process

Build, Don't Extract

Every engagement should leave communities, partners, and markets stronger than we found them. We choose clients and projects that create lasting value, not just short term returns.

Win Win or No Deal

We have built our entire network on mutually beneficial relationships. If a deal doesn't work for everyone at the table, it doesn't work for us. This is how trust is built and sustained.

Relationships Over Transactions

We build lasting strategic partnerships, not one off consulting engagements. Every relationship in our network has been developed over years of genuine trust and delivered results.

Honesty Over Sales

If we can't help you, we'll say so. If a market isn't right for your business, we'll tell you. If an opportunity has risks, you'll hear about them before you invest.

Generational Thinking

We partner with companies that are serious about building sustainable operations, not those looking for a quick entry and exit. African markets reward patience, persistence, and commitment.

Execution Over Strategy

We don't produce reports that sit on shelves. We build relationships, solve problems, source deals, and make things happen on the ground. Results, not documents.
