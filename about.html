Nana Adjei Sarpong

Founder and Principal Consultant

Ionya exists because I saw the same problem over and over: international companies with genuine ambitions in Africa, stuck because they didn't have the right relationships. They'd hire big consultancies who'd deliver a strategy deck and disappear. The real work, navigating government ministries, building trust with local partners, solving the operational crises that happen at 2am, that requires someone who's actually on the ground.

I built Ionya to be the partner I wish existed for these companies. We're based in Ghana, but our network extends across the continent. When a client needs access to a new market, we build the relationships. When they face a challenge, we solve it through our network. And when there's an investment opportunity worth pursuing, we find it before it hits the open market.

We don't work with everyone. We choose our clients carefully, because the depth of partnership we provide only works when there's genuine alignment. If we can help, we'll go further than any consultancy you've worked with. If we can't, we'll tell you upfront.

How We Operate

Principles, not just process

Relationships Over Transactions

We build lasting strategic partnerships, not one off consulting engagements. Every relationship in our network has been developed over years of genuine trust.

Honesty Over Sales

If we can't help you, we'll say so. If a market isn't right for your business, we'll tell you. If an opportunity has risks, you'll hear about them before you invest.

Network Over Knowledge

Anyone can research African markets. What they can't do is pick up the phone and reach a government minister, a concession holder, or the exact local partner you need.

Quality Over Volume

We work with a select number of clients at any given time. This ensures every partner gets the full depth of our network and attention, not a diluted version of it.

Execution Over Strategy

We don't produce reports that sit on shelves. We build relationships, solve problems, source deals, and make things happen on the ground.

Long Term Over Quick Wins

African markets reward patience and persistence. We partner with companies that are serious about building sustainable operations, not those looking for a quick entry and exit.
